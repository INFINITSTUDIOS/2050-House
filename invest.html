How are referrals tracked?

Your link carries your code (?ref=CODE). When a visitor arrives through it, the code is remembered on their device and attached to their RSVP, ticket or sponsorship inquiry. Buyers can also enter your code by hand.

When are rewards paid?

Rewards are reconciled after each event closes and paid out within 30 days, with a report showing every referred sale.

Can I refer other sponsors?

Yes. When a company you refer signs a sponsorship, you get a one-time bonus as a percentage of their package value.

Is this an investment in Feedia?

No. Sponsorships buy event visibility and benefits, and affiliate rewards are performance-based. This is not an offer of equity or securities. For investment conversations, book a meeting.
